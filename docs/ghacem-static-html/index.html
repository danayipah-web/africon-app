The Cement Behind Ghana’s Growth Since 1967

If someone is building a house, running a block factory, laying a road, or constructing a commercial complex in Ghana, GHACEM is the name they rely on. Established in 1967 and part of the global Heidelberg Materials family, we supply standard and specialty cements engineered for reliability, accessible pricing, and dependable delivery to every corner of the nation.
