What you can do

Bookings

Search, filter and export bookings, create and edit them, move people between events, and see booking and revenue trends.

CRM

Every customer and lead in one list. Build audiences with conditions, save segments, import a CSV and compose email or SMS campaigns.

Events & Trainings

Create events with the full 11-step setup, track places and payments, mark attendance and message attendees.

Dashboard

Bookings, active events and revenue at a glance, plus a feed of what your team changed.

Users & roles

Admin, Content Manager and Viewer each see exactly what their role allows.

Light and dark

Follows your phone's theme, or pick one in Settings.

Install it in four steps

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.Tap the button above, or scan the QR code from a computer.
  2. Open the downloaded file.Find it in your notifications or the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install.Android asks to let your browser or file manager "install unknown apps". Allow it for this install. Play Protect may warn that the app is unrecognised; choose "Install anyway".
  4. Open Africon Admin and sign inwith one of the demo accounts below.

This is a test build. All data stays on your phone, and payments, email and SMS are simulated: nothing is charged or sent.
